Chemistry You Can Trust,

Founded over two decades ago, Rangechem has grown from a specialist chemical formulator into one of the Western Cape's leading suppliers of industrial cleaning solutions. Our in-house laboratory continuously develops and refines formulations to meet the demands of modern industry.

We are committed to providing products that are highly effective and competitively priced for every scale of operation — from small businesses to large industrial facilities.
