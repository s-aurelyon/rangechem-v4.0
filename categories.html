Our Product Ranges

Three core ranges engineered for industrial performance — formulated and packaged at our Western Cape facility.

Range 01

Detergents

Professional-grade cleaning compounds for surfaces, floors, glass, laundry and dishwashing. Our pH-balanced formulations are engineered to be safe for high-frequency industrial use while delivering consistent, powerful results.

Suitable for manufacturing facilities, hospitality operations, educational institutions and any environment that demands reliable daily cleaning performance.

  • Multi-surface & floor cleaners
  • Commercial dishwash liquids
  • Industrial laundry compounds
  • Window & glass cleaners
  • Concentrate & RTU formats

Range 02

Degreasers

Heavy-duty solvent and aqueous degreasers designed to dissolve oils, lubricants and carbon deposits from machinery, engines and processing equipment. Our formulations cut through the toughest industrial soiling with minimal effort.

Trusted by automotive workshops, manufacturing plants, food processing facilities and engineering companies across the Western Cape.

  • Citrus & solvent-based degreasers
  • Engine & gearbox cleaners
  • Kitchen & extraction degreasers
  • Cold parts cleaning solutions
  • Alkaline foam degreasers
View Degreasers Range
Range 03

Disinfectants

Hospital-grade and industrial disinfectants and sanitisers that eliminate bacteria, fungi and viruses on contact — compliant with SA health standards. Our range covers surface disinfection, hand hygiene and large-scale fogging applications.

Used by healthcare facilities, food processing plants, government buildings and hospitality businesses that require verified, consistent pathogen control.

  • Surface & floor disinfectants
  • Hand sanitiser solutions
  • Fogging & spray disinfectants
  • Hospital & food-safe grades
  • Concentrate & RTU formats
